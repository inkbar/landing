What you can build with Inkbar

Jobs your users do with PDFs, done inside your app instead of in another program. Each runs in the browser and is free under the MIT licence.

  • app.example.com/customers/C-30918/redact
    9:30
    app.example.com

    Let people redact PDFs without leaving your app

    Mark text and areas, then remove what lies under them and check the file it wrote.

  • app.example.com/membership/apply
    9:30
    app.example.com

    Let people fill in PDF forms inside your app

    Fill in text, checkboxes, lists and signature fields, with the form's own scripts running.

  • app.example.com/letters/tenancy-renewal/sign
    9:30
    app.example.com

    Let people sign PDFs in your app

    Draw, type or upload a signature, then place it on a page or in a signature field.

  • app.example.com/handbook/review
    9:30
    app.example.com

    Review and comment on PDFs in your app

    Highlight passages, pin notes and reply in threads, stored in your own backend.

Install it and see.

You don't need a trial or a sign-up form. Open the sandbox to try it on your own PDF, which never leaves your browser, or follow the quickstart to put a document in your app.

$pnpm add @inkbar/react @inkbar/core @inkbar/theme
